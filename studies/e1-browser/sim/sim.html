E1 JSBSim worker benchmark

Study page (preliminary evidence). Runs the JSBSim WASM worker on a scripted input log: two pre-scheduled replays (step cost, pacing, determinism) and one live run (input-to-state latency). Query parameters: seconds, live, state_hz. Aircraft data: JSBSim c172x (generic 1982 172P model), not a validated 172S/FR172.
