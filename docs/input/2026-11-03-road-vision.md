# Expanded roadmap vision, 2026-11-03

User roadmap note, preserved verbatim with escaped spaces decoded:

```text
ROAD:

vision:

- the most a realistic flying experience with minimum prerequisites -- in the browser,
- different experience levels, from just fly to exercises for PPL and other small plane licenses
- explicitly include instrument and procedures and connect to professional material

community:

- planes flying real-time
- plane models contributing
- any of the world and model data below
- contributions and suggestions / forum

partnerships:

- from ecosystem, as long as the platform stays open
- content: maps providers, publishers
- awareness: ATC,plane manufacturers
- usage: flight schools

financing:

- free version stays free
- potentially premium variant that uses commercial maps / subscriptions (eg, Navigraph)
- AI-based extensions in premium to cover tokens
- extend partners

realistic weather conditions:

- weather and reporting, AI and real
- cloud cover, rain, fog
- temperature simulation

realistic traffic conditions:

- planes, AI and community
- NOTAM
- restricted areas and no-fly zones
- eg, trying to land on a busy intl. airport

navigation:

- maps with restricted areas and controlled zones from real data, see world realism below
- select nav aids from map
- define gps markers
- flight plan with lookup of fixes
- flight planning, incl. glide slope
- project map markers (towns, landmarks, streets, rivers, air fixes) onto terrain.
- realistic nav aids, eg VOR tuning, deviation, DME slant distance, ILS glideslope geometry

communications:

- com1,2 communication simulation with airports, AI
- com1,2 stations like nav1,2 stations

controls:

- avionics as HUD
- no-panel mode: transparent main instruments.
- connect iphone or android and orientation mode
- realistic instruments, eg, magnetic north
- check lists lookup, run through

world realism:

- now hessen and surrounding federal states
- focus on open DACH data
- later extension to interesting areas with open data sources -- OurAirports, OpenFlightMaps, pot. Navigraph, Eurocontrol / EAD, later FAA and other areas
- discuss idea of flying through Google Earth
- add procedural reconstruction, semantic reconstruction, or geotypical scenery generation
```

This record preserves product directions and candidate services. It does not
record a provider, financing arrangement, partnership or implementation as selected
or delivered.
