## Verified operating facts

The fit is **two axis with optional altitude preselect**, not the visually similar
single-axis unit. Its altitude-selector/ARM/BARO controls identify the additional
fit. K1 pp.2–3,83–89; C1 S15-4,12–13. For software 03/01 onward, engagement is by
holding AP at least 0.25 s. Earlier HDG/ALT buttons can engage AP; that must not be
carried into the later variant. C1 identifies later behavior at 172S9129 onward,
or earlier 172S8348–9128 incorporating KC140-M1. K1 p.83; C1 S15-8–9.

| Control/state | Source-backed meaning | Reference |

### Lateral course and capture inputs

The installed DG matters. Its heading bug supplies the course datum during
NAV/APR/REV. OBS establishes the VOR course, but the pilot must also put the DG bug
on that course; for REV use the **front-course inbound** heading. Mode selection
flashes HDG for 5 s as a reminder. Arming from HDG computes a 45° intercept;
arming from ROL retains the pilot-established intercept. With an HSI, its course
pointer supplies course and the heading bug supplies the chosen intercept heading.
Do not silently implement HSI behavior behind a DG face. C1 S15-11,29–35;
K1 pp.14–31.

Outside approximately 2–3 CDI dots, lateral capture is armed until a computed
capture point; inside that range the capture sequence starts upon selection.
The sources give neither an exact threshold within that band nor a capture law.
APR localizer tracking automatically arms GS with valid NAV1 ILS. GS centering
captures from **above or below**, replaces ALT with GS, and inhibits altitude
preselect capture. C1 S15-30,32,34–35; K1 100.

### Startup, failures and override

Power-up performs an internal PFT sequence before engagement is permitted. Successful
test lights display segments and sounds disconnect audio; C1 includes external
pitch-trim annunciation. No exact PFT step count/duration is published here. A
temporary P warning around 30 s is described. C1 S15-4,20–21; K1 p.8.

AP disconnect gives flashing AP for 5 s and a 2 s tone. Installed disconnect/trim
interrupt and manual electric trim disconnect AP; power, turn-coordinator or
monitored system failures also remove it. K1 p.85; C1 S15-4–5,10.

Flagged HDG/NAV/APR/REV flashes the affected mode and has already fallen back to
ROL; its button clears the flash. GS loss flashes GS and falls back to VS;
valid GS automatically rearms, and rapid ALT twice clears the flash. These differ
from the normal 5 s DG course reminder. Blind-encoder loss makes altitude
alerter/preselect unavailable. The DG itself has no validity flag; a simulator
must not invent original automatic detection of every DG failure. C1 S15-18–19.

Physical control-wheel overpower is checked during preflight, but hand-flying
against engaged AP is prohibited and trim can oppose the force. Automatic raw
joystick/yoke-input disconnect is therefore an explicit simulator substitution,
not a hardware claim. C1 S15-14,23,26. CWS is optional in the generic K1 system
diagram; this C172 supplement does not establish an installed CWS control.

## Inferences, limitations and implementation handoff

- **Inference:** changing BARO should change selected-altitude/alert conversion,
  without moving an already captured pressure-altitude reference: K1 p.87 says
  pressure-altitude hold and p.84 separates it from the displayed alerter target.
  Neither source explicitly walks through a live BARO-change example.
- Knob detent size, barometric setting range, selected-altitude range, internal
  capture gains/anticipation, VS rounding and precise button-repeat timing are
  not established. Do not cite arbitrary simulator bounds/tuning as manufacturer
  values. The manual's approximately 10%-of-VS transient altitude overshoot is a
  description, not a permissible invented controller tolerance.
- C1 S15-14 documents operational limits: off for takeoff/landing; 70–140 KIAS,
  approach at least 80 KIAS; flaps at most 10°; disengage below 200 ft AGL on
  approach and 800 ft AGL otherwise. These are pilot limitations, **not evidence
  of automatic envelope protection or interlocks**. S15-33/35 separately recommends
  90 KIAS for coupled approaches. Research does not establish certified model
  behavior outside the envelope.
- Later KLN94 roll steering is a separate integration (172S9423 onward; C1
  S15-5,24,31). This note does not establish GPS installation, flight-plan
  sequencing or roll-steering fidelity in the prototype.
- The prototype's true-heading reference and fictional LAB VOR need explicit
  teaching labels. No real station/procedure, live localizer/glideslope signal,
  magnetic compass model, physical servos/trim force or aircraft-calibrated
  KAP 140 controller is established by these sources. Functional mode tests can
  use declared synthetic signals; test fixtures do not create a real ILS.
- Reuse of generic native servos can demonstrate these controls, arming and
  response. It cannot establish original installed response tolerances, training
  credit or validated KAP 140 flight performance. Source transcription and
  aircraft-installation uncertainty remain visible.
