# Airliner feasibility: 737, A320 and MD-80

Research checked 2026-11-04 by Codex subagent `glide_housekeeping`. Scope: source availability, cockpit/system reuse and platform fit; no aircraft, assets, SDK or flight model installed or accepted. Vendor realism statements are claims, not quantitative validation.

## Product and variant boundaries

| Candidate | Verified current scope | Gefly implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [PMDG 737-800](https://pmdg.com/pmdg-737-800-for-microsoft-flight-simulator-2024/) | 737-800 **NG**, native MSFS 2024 PC product; separate simulator purchase and online operation required. | Not a MAX or a browser/Mac aircraft. Good reference/partnership candidate; retail purchase is not reusable physics, cockpit artwork or source. |
| [Fenix A320](https://fenixsim.com/a320/) | A320-200 **ceo**, CFM56-5B4 and IAE V2527-A5 variants; Windows 10/11 and MSFS 2020/2024. | [FlyByWire A32NX](https://github.com/flybywiresim/aircraft/blob/master/README.md) | Open-source project targeting **A320-251N**, LEAP-1A26, with identified avionics configuration, inside Microsoft Flight Simulator. | Strongest named code-reuse lead, but simulator runtime and mixed assets prevent a turnkey standalone browser port. |
| [Leonardo Fly the Maddog](https://www.flythemaddog.com/site/msfs-/) | Current MSFS 2020/2024 package includes **MD-82, MD-83 and MD-88**. [Features](https://www.flythemaddog.com/site/features/) identify JT8D-217A/C versus JT8D-219 engines and selectable FMS/EFIS fits. | “MD-80” is insufficient as an implementation target. Select one variant, engine and cockpit fit; do not combine options into an imaginary panel. |

These commercial simulator routes require an external desktop simulator. A browser control client or streamed view would not make the aircraft run natively in Gefly or remove Windows, hardware, account and licence requirements. Microsoft's [SimConnect documentation](https://docs.flightsimulator.com/html/Programming_Tools/SimConnect/SimConnect_SDK.htm) describes components communicating with the simulator, including native executables and in-simulator WASM. That WASM is not evidence of a standalone web-compatible aircraft.

## Rights and interfaces

**PMDG:** its [current EULA](https://pmdg.com/end-user-license-agreements/) restricts use to private entertainment, excludes preparation for aircraft qualification/training/currency, prohibits redistribution and requires written permission for modifying/reusing supplied material. A published [737 introduction](https://downloads.pmdg.com/supportfiles/Docs/PMDG_737_MSFS_Introduction.pdf) also limits the manual to the simulation and prohibits duplication. Public vendor-forum [SDK documentation references](https://forum.pmdg.com/forum/main-forum/pmdg-737-for-msfs/model-animations-documentation/294164-were-can-i-find-documentation-and-activate-data-broadcast) identify installed broadcast documentation; full current SDK terms/coverage were not inspected. External switch/display access is not a grant to redistribute its dynamics or copy its panel assets.

**Fenix:** [consumer terms](https://fenixsim.com/assets/docs/2024-terms-and-conditions.pdf), clauses 6.3–6.8, restrict retail use to private entertainment, exclude professional training/educational use, limit cockpit connectivity and require a separate agreement for commercial use; adaptation/source extraction is restricted. A future Gefly school, educational service or external bridge must resolve those rights first. Current public SDK coverage was not established; do not promise every switch/display is externally controllable.

**Maddog:** vendor feature pages cover systems, cockpit controls, manuals, FMS options and subscription-based chart integration. A complete public EULA/SDK redistribution or training grant was not located in this bounded review. That uncertainty is not permission to extract its flight model or assets; obtain applicable terms and written integration rights before a partnership/bridge commitment. No proprietary package was downloaded.

**FlyByWire:** [licensing guidance](https://www.flybywiresim.com/licenses/) and the [actual GPLv3 text](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/flybywiresim/aircraft/master/LICENSE) distinguish original code from original 3D assets under **CC BY-NC 4.0**, Microsoft-derived content under Game Content Usage Rules, MSFS SDK obligations and third-party file licences. GPL code may be a compatible starting point after file-level review; the complete distribution is not uniformly GPL. Noncommercial assets are not cleared for an unrestricted free/open baseline with future paid services. Replace or separately license restricted assets; isolate/rework simulator-variable, flight-control and display dependencies. Navigation datasets, charts, fonts and sounds require their own inventory; an individual chart subscription does not grant Gefly redistribution rights.

## Other open-source leads

FlightGear's [A320-family project](https://github.com/legoboyvdlp/A320-family) contains flight dynamics, systems, Nasal scripts and cockpit resources. Its wiki labels GPLv2+, but current [copyright notice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/legoboyvdlp/A320-family/dev/README.copyright.md) grants GPL **version 2** without a later-version clause. Resolve that discrepancy and per-file compatibility with Gefly GPLv3 before combining code. FlightGear/Canvas/Nasal dependencies also need porting, not just XML loading.

The upstream [JSBSim 737 XML](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JSBSim-Team/jsbsim/master/aircraft/737/737.xml) is directly inspectable, but declares beta status, admits guessed/unvalidated data and combines a GPL declaration with a no-sale notice. It is not PMDG, not an identified NG/MAX package, and not a rights/fidelity-cleared shortcut. FlightGear lists [737-300](https://wiki.flightgear.org/Boeing_737-300) and [MD-80](https://wiki.flightgear.org/Aircraft) candidates; a current MD-80 source/licence/FDM audit was not completed. Availability in a hangar alone establishes neither complete systems nor reusable asset rights.

## Feasible paths and next decision

1. **Browser-native open implementation:** select one exact aircraft fit, audit reusable code/assets, preserve Gefly's JSBSim worker boundary and build missing systems/displays. Best baseline alignment; highest implementation/data-validation burden. Start with one genuine systems-plus-flight slice, not a new aircraft selection label over C172 physics.
2. **Optional external Windows bridge:** licensed local MSFS/add-on installation remains authoritative; Gefly can expose permitted controls, views or exercises through a narrowly defined interface. Requires demonstrated API coverage, latency/failure handling and appropriate use rights. It does not satisfy browser-only operation or a portable open aircraft model.
3. **Vendor/manufacturer partnership:** negotiate educational/commercial rights, source or SDK access, data applicability and validation evidence. No agreement, cost or access is assumed.

For the requested realistic **all controls/instruments and dynamics**, first freeze variant, engine, avionics standard and options. Inventory every control/display/state dependency through cold-and-dark, normal operations and defined failures. Separately compare sourced performance and response across mass/CG, configuration, altitude, engines and selected abnormal regimes: takeoff/landing, climb, fuel/thrust and control-law/transient behavior, with stated tolerances and missing-data limits. Manuals and retail screenshots do not supply complete aerodynamic tables or permission to reproduce them. No candidate has met those criteria in Gefly; no implementation effort or training credit is accepted by this study.
