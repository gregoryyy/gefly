## Source-backed distinctions

K1 pp.84–86: selecting an altitude arms capture; it does not command a climb.
From ALT, selecting ALT again enters VS with current vertical speed. When the VS
command is hidden, the first UP/DN press reveals it; further presses adjust
100FPM, or roughly 300FPM/s when held. The shared numeric display normally shows
selected altitude, switching to commanded VS for three seconds after adjustment.
In ALT, UP/DN instead nudges the hidden hold reference 20ft per tap; holding
requests 500FPM and release adopts actual altitude (pp.84,87).

K1 p.3 lists CWS as **optional** across variants. Page86 explicitly keeps the VS
command visible during CWS. Page89 mentions CWS during a trim check, but the
inspected Honeywell guide does **not** establish a complete CWS servo-release,
mode-retention or release-recapture algorithm.

D1 9-A13-36 does document generic KAP140 CWS: holding it releases pitch, roll
and pitch-trim clutches for manual maneuvering; release synchronizes ALT or VS
commands to actual altitude or vertical speed. It separately states that manual
electric trim disengages AP. This supports generic CWS clutch/synchronization
semantics, while leaving exact 172S installation and calibrated response unverified.

C1 S15-13 confirms separate target/ARM and temporary VS display. S15-27 specifies
VS commands between −2000 and +1500FPM and requires a climb/descent toward the
selected altitude; capability depends on aircraft power/weight. S15-26 warns
against hand-flying against engaged autopilot. Its described wheel control is
A/P DISC/TRIM INT; this supplement does not attest an installed CWS button in
the selected 172S fit.

## Implementation boundary

Do not make altitude selection silently change ALT into VS or reinterpret ALT
nudges as a sustained selected-altitude climb. Explain the active mode/display
and measure the native climb/capture response; matching labels does not calibrate
the generic JSBSim controller or establish manufacturer gains/tolerances.

A hold-to-borrow CWS control can follow D1 as an explicitly declared optional
simulator fit. Retained lateral/GS details and manual-input interactions need a
documented simulator contract and tests; the sources do not establish stock-172S
CWS equipment or response fidelity. Keep CWS distinct from AP disconnection and
retain read-only recorded indications.
