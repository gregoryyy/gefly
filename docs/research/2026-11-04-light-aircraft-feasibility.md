# Light-aircraft feasibility: dynamics and complete cockpits

Research snapshot: 4 November 2026. This assesses **quantitative whole-envelope dynamics and realistic operation of every fitted cockpit control/system**, not merely a flyable visual model. No aircraft integration, licence clearance or validation was performed. Public manuals support procedures, limitations and performance reference points; they rarely supply the aerodynamic, propulsion and failure-state data needed to establish that objective.

## Pipistrel Velis Electro

The certified aircraft is **Virus SW128**, marketed as Velis Electro, not Alpha Electro. Bind an implementation to the applicable serial-number configuration and AFM revision: the [EASA type-certificate record](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/type-certificates/aircraft-cs-25-cs-22-cs-23-cs-vla-cs-lsa/easaa573-virus-sw-121) and [UK TCDS, Issue 1, 8 June 2022](https://www.caa.co.uk/Documents/Download/3934/ca692457-336e-4190-840e-cd26cf498935/3180) identify the certified combination; development-era motor ratings must not replace approved operating limits.

The [manufacturer](https://www.pipistrel-aircraft.com/products/velis-electro/) identifies the EPSI 570C propulsion display. The manufacturer-authored [POH C01, 1 February 2022, operator-hosted](https://old.santamonicaflyers.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/POH-Velis-Pilot-operating-handbook.pdf) describes the E-811 liquid-cooled motor, H300C controller, two HV battery packs, BMS/contactors, fixed-pitch propeller, auxiliary electrical supply and cooling. Master/avionics, BATT EN/PWR EN, power lever, trim, flaps, brakes, EPSI indications/annunciations and the installed radio/EFIS form a tractable control inventory. Instrument options require their own manuals; a generic glass display is not an EPSI implementation.

There is unusually useful reuse: upstream [FGAddon AlphaElectro](https://svn.code.sf.net/p/flightgear/fgaddon/trunk/Aircraft/AlphaElectro/) contains separate `veliselectro-jsb.xml`, `veliselectro-set.xml` and Nasal battery/electrical/cooling logic. Its [README](https://svn.code.sf.net/p/flightgear/fgaddon/trunk/Aircraft/AlphaElectro/README.md) grants GPLv2-or-later; individual dependencies/assets still need an inventory. The actual [Velis FDM](https://svn.code.sf.net/p/flightgear/fgaddon/trunk/Aircraft/AlphaElectro/veliselectro-jsb.xml), version 08/2020, is marked **ALPHA**, with explicit geometric TODOs. Existing code and apparent cockpit completeness are not validation evidence.

Request current AFM/equipment/software definitions plus motor torque-speed/efficiency maps, controller current/voltage limits and derating, battery capacity/OCV/resistance versus SOC, temperature and ageing, thermal masses/cooling maps and fault transitions. SOC/SOH/range display descriptions alone cannot establish energy or thermal accuracy. Reuse JSBSim aerodynamics and assess this FlightGear electric subsystem before porting it deterministically; do not invent a battery model from nominal endurance.

## Extra 330SC and 330LX: two integrations

[330SC](https://extraaircraft.com/330sc/) is single-seat; [330LX](https://extraaircraft.com/330lx/) is tandem two-seat. Both manufacturer pages list AEIO-580-B1A and MTV9-B-C/C198-25 propulsion, but geometry, loading, electrical installations and cockpit arrangements differ. Their official type/manual identifiers are **EA300/SC** and **EA300/LC**, respectively; [Extra’s publication catalog](https://extraaircraft.com/tech-service/) lists separate manuals and registration-controlled access. Neither an Extra 300L nor a 330LT/SX is interchangeable evidence.

Both need throttle/mixture/propeller governor, fuel selection/pumps, trim, electrical and engine instruments, and optional smoke/avionics systems. The LX additionally needs correct duplicated controls and indications. Request the applicable AFM/supplements, engine/propeller/governor manuals, mass/inertia versus loading and OEM/flight-test coefficients across positive and negative angle of attack and sideslip. High-rate coupling, propeller gyroscopic effects, snap rolls, spins and inverted fuel/oil behaviour are major additional requirements. Published g limits and aerobatic capability do not specify those dynamics.

No exact, licence-verified open 330SC or 330LX package was established in this bounded search; upstream FGAddon’s `extra500` is unrelated. Retain JSBSim, but require suitable OEM/test data and aerobatic specialist review before claiming quantitative aerobatic fidelity. Treat each Extra as a separate configuration, not a panel skin.

## Pitts S-1S: establish the individual aircraft first

[Aviat’s S-1S description](https://aviataircraft.com/aviat-aircraft-pitts/) identifies the certified single-seat 180 hp aircraft and Sensenich fixed-pitch propeller. Its [technical-publication catalog](https://aviataircraft.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/Pitts_Technical_Publications.pdf) lists S-1S flight manual 70198-001, Revision C, 29 October 1976; this is a catalog reference, not assurance that it is the latest applicable material. Do not substitute the S-1T’s engine/constant-speed installation or the S-2’s two-seat geometry.

“Pitts S-1S” also describes amateur-built aircraft with materially different engines, props, rigging and panels. The regulator’s [G-IIIX investigation](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5422f3b240f0b613460004e3/dft_avsafety_pdf_501242.pdf), for example, identifies an O-360-A4A installation. Select certified type/serial/STCs or one documented amateur-built configuration before listing controls. A carburettor, injected engine and propeller governor cannot be represented by the same control logic.

Obtain applicable approved/aircraft-specific manuals, engine/propeller and inverted-system documentation, actual rigging/inertia and aerodynamic/spin data. Biplane interference, post-stall behaviour and tailwheel ground handling are substantial gaps. Upstream FlightGear has `pittss1c`; that is **not** evidence of an exact S-1S model. No exact source/licence-verified S-1S package was established here.

## Piper Warrior and Arrow: keep the variants separate

A useful first target is **PA-28-161 Warrior II, 160 hp**, with carburetted O-320-D3G, fixed-pitch propeller and fixed gear. The manufacturer-authored [VB-1180 POH, issued 13 August 1982, operator-hosted](https://www.chestercountyaviation.com/_files/ugd/c1fec2_1a38c5192f3743e8932e08dda1af8181.pdf) supplies configuration-specific operating/performance references. Do not substitute PA-28-151, Archer PA-28-181 or a 180 hp conversion.

For **PA-28R-201 Arrow III**, select the conventional-tail, normally aspirated fuel-injected IO-360-C1C6 configuration: [manufacturer-authored VB-1612 POH, issued 12 July 1995, instructor-hosted](https://www.jasonblair.net/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/Piper-Arrow-PA28R-201.pdf). Its constant-speed governor, retractable gear/electrohydraulics, warnings/emergency extension and alternate-air system add real work. Bind automatic-extension equipment to the individual serial/supplements. Arrow IV T-tail and turbo Arrow are different models. Obtain current applicable material through [Piper technical publications](https://www.piper.com/technical-publications/).

Both need faithful fuel selection/pumps, ignition/starting, electrical buses/circuit breakers, pitot/static/vacuum instruments, manual flaps, stabilator/anti-servo trim and brakes. Warrior carburettor heat differs from Arrow alternate air; fitted radios/autopilots need their own manuals. Published cruise/climb/stall points leave stability derivatives, control effectiveness, inertia, propeller off-design and stall/spin behaviour unresolved.

Upstream [FGAddon PA28](https://svn.code.sf.net/p/flightgear/fgaddon/trunk/Aircraft/PA28/) contains `PA28-161-160.xml`, an identified Warrior II FDM, and detailed systems/cockpit code. Its `LICENSE` is GPLv2 text; confirm any “or later” permission and per-asset terms before incorporation into this GPLv3 project. JSBSim’s [bundled pa28.xml](https://github.com/JSBSim-Team/jsbsim/blob/master/aircraft/pa28/pa28.xml) identifies **PA28-180**, not either target. No exact Arrow III reuse package was established here.

## Recommendation and remaining boundary

Relative complexity is an engineering judgment, not measured effort: Warrior routine-PPL systems/dynamics is the most straightforward; Arrow adds propulsion/gear complexity; Velis has good reuse but difficult energy/thermal validation; both Extras and Pitts have the hardest whole-envelope aerodynamic requirement. None currently has demonstrated coverage of the stated objective.

Keep JSBSim and separate deterministic aircraft systems from browser instruments. FlightGear XML can aid FDM reuse; Nasal/Canvas systems do not automatically execute in Gefly’s worker. First request exact configurations, redistribution rights and validation datasets; then audit one reusable package against its manuals and measured reference cases. Manuals and consumer add-ons grant neither aerodynamic completeness nor source/asset reuse rights. Link copyrighted manuals rather than copying their drawings. Missing whole-envelope evidence remains a fidelity limit, even when every cockpit button works.
