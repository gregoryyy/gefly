## What the open models actually provide

The official [FGAddon ASK21 tree](https://svn.code.sf.net/p/flightgear/fgaddon/trunk/Aircraft/ASK21/) contains `ask21-set.xml` selecting YASim and [`ask21-jsb-set.xml`](https://svn.code.sf.net/p/flightgear/fgaddon/trunk/Aircraft/ASK21/ask21-jsb-set.xml) selecting `jsb`/`ask21-jsb`. Its [README licensing section](https://svn.code.sf.net/p/flightgear/fgaddon/trunk/Aircraft/ASK21/README.md) expressly grants GPL version2 or later. [`ask21-jsb.xml`](https://svn.code.sf.net/p/flightgear/fgaddon/trunk/Aircraft/ASK21/ask21-jsb.xml) declares Aeromatic0.96 and `release="ALPHA"`; generated coefficients are a starting hypothesis, not manufacturer measurements. The set file names aerotow/winch external-force hitches; FlightGear/Nasal launch integration does not automatically transfer into Gefly's standalone WASM worker.

The [LS4 tree](https://svn.code.sf.net/p/flightgear/fgaddon/trunk/Aircraft/LS4/) includes GPLv2 [`COPYING`](https://svn.code.sf.net/p/flightgear/fgaddon/trunk/Aircraft/LS4/COPYING), separate JSBSim set files, [`references.md`](https://svn.code.sf.net/p/flightgear/fgaddon/trunk/Aircraft/LS4/references.md) and polar/CG calculation material. A later-version grant and per-asset exceptions were not established; clarify before combining with Gefly's GPLv3 distribution. `ls4b.xml` describes LS4b but retains an LS4a configuration-name header: audit variants rather than trusting labels. [G103's actual set file](https://svn.code.sf.net/p/flightgear/fgaddon/trunk/Aircraft/G103/g103-twin-astir-ii-set.xml) and FDM identify Twin II; an exact original-model license was not verified.

Inspection hashes: ASK21 `ask21-jsb.xml` SHA256 `36ed82c4051a62c7a7c13c4dbb5221ce488b121bd7ec901ad18ea37237f92b27`; LS4 `ls4b.xml` SHA256 `98be7633fd0e21274f1f6808b855029dd4aa8f1ecc70d39ffea1fd585fb7bd09`. Trunk URLs are mutable; adoption needs a pinned revision and a complete asset/license inventory. No X-Plane/MSFS/Condor aircraft was verified here as redistributable open FDM/source; availability for download is insufficient.

## Requirements for realistic flight and complete cockpits

AFM/MM sources can establish limits, loading/CG, geometry, rigging/control travel, systems and selected polars. A sink-speed polar constrains steady performance; it does **not** uniquely provide lift/drag over angle-of-attack, sideslip, angular-rate derivatives, stall hysteresis, spin entry/recovery or control hinge moments. Request applicable OEM aerodynamic/inertia/control-force data and permission, then arrange instrumented validation with qualified pilots. Initial support can cover measured steady flight, airbrake effects and loading; do not advertise validated spins from a fitted polar.

Tow/winch need attachment-point forces, coupled tug/winch motion, tension/slack, elasticity/damping, release/weak-link behavior and launch transitions. Airbrake drag/pitch, adverse yaw/slips, ground handling and ballast mass/CG/drain effects need aircraft-specific evidence. ASK21 spin ballast is not generic water ballast. Original Twin Astir and LS4 ballast/gear installations must follow their exact configuration. Keyboard/touchpad cannot reproduce physical stick/pedal forces; credible force feedback additionally needs hinge-moment/linkage/friction data and suitable hardware.

“All controls/instruments” requires an owner-approved cockpit definition: front/rear differences, stick/pedals/trim, airbrake and wheel-brake coupling, release, canopy/vent, gear and ballast controls where fitted; exact ASI/altimeter/compass, pneumatic/TE variometer plumbing, slip string, radio, flight computer/FLARM and electrical fit. Owner panels vary. Manufacturer [Winter instrument information](https://www.winter-instruments.de/stauscheiben-variometer-vanetype-vario) and [LXNAV S100 manuals](https://gliding.lxnav.com/products/s100/docs/) support identified equipment, not a universal glider panel. Obtain permitted photos/dimensions and implement authored controls; do not copy scanned instrument artwork or firmware.

Recommendation: preserve the current C172 delivery focus; subsequently investigate **one ASK21 configuration deeply**, then LS4-b. Defer original Twin Astir until its data/rights gaps close. Separate steady-flight, launch, advanced handling and complete-system acceptance; this is a proposed sequence, not a roadmap commitment or training-credit claim.
