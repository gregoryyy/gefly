## Question and method

Reims FR172 ("Reims Rocket") sub-models, their engines, propellers and certified
limits, and what rights apply to those sources? User direction (GAPS Q3, 2026-11-01
07:04/07:08): classic-panel 172S first, FR172 second, as distinct engine/propeller
configurations, using public FAA (and where needed EASA/DGAC) documentation.

## Extracted facts (all from search snippets unless marked verified)

### Cessna 172S

- Type certificate: FAA **3A12** covers the 172S; TC holder now Textron Aviation. Current
  revision is 88 or 89; date not confirmed (S1). *Unverified.*
- Engine: **Lycoming IO-360-L2A, 180 hp, 2,700 rpm** for all operations (3A12 excerpt via
  S1/S7; consistent with 1E10/IM.E.032 power table showing IO-360-L2A at 180 hp, S8/S9).
  The same engine model also appears at **160 hp** (172R and kit-modified installations),
  so the rating must be tied to the 172S entry. *Unverified.*
- Maximum weight, normal category: **2,550 lb (1,156 kg)** (3A12 footnote and NZ TAR
  snippets). *Unverified.*
- Airspeed limits: Vne **163 KIAS (160 KCAS)**, Vno **129 KIAS (126 KCAS)**; reported by a
  secondary page that cites 3A12 Rev 89 (S1). Va, Vfe not obtained for the 172S. *Unverified.*
- Propeller: a 3A12 copy (Rev 85, via Scribd) lists **McCauley 1A170E/JHA7660**, fixed
  pitch, diameter 75–76 in, static rpm 2,300–2,400 at full throttle; the snippet layout was
  garbled and a Rev 88 snippet listed a different McCauley entry. *Unverified; conflict.*
- Serial eligibility: UK CAA snippet reads "17280001 and on", probably a transcription of
  **172S8001 and on** (S7). The NZ TAR's "172S11615 and on" appears to mark the Textron
  Aviation holder era, not an avionics change. *Unverified.*
- G1000 transition: Cessna announced G1000 for the 172 at AirVenture 2004; type
  certification of the G1000-equipped 172S reported April 2005 (General Aviation News,
  2005-04-08; FlightGlobal). At launch G1000 was described as optional ("NAV III") on the
  SP. Listings show G1000 172S at s/n 172S10163 (2006) and 172S10863 (2008). **No source
  found giving the first G1000 or last conventional-panel serial.** A CAP guide notes its
  non-G1000 172S are mostly model years 1999–2001 (fleet-specific). *Unverified.*
- 172S introduction as model year 1998 is from general knowledge, not a source read here.

### Reims FR172 (Reims Rocket)

- Type certificate: FAA **A18EU** covers FR172E through FR172K, TC holder Reims Aviation
  S.A. (RGL listing, S2; Cessna Flyer and STC AMLs). Revision 7 vs 8 conflict. Basis listed
  as CAR 3 with FAR 21.29 (import). *Unverified.* The US-built counterpart R172E–R172K is on
  **3A17** (Cessna 175 lineage) (S3). The standard Reims F172 is on **A4EU** (S4) and
  is a different configuration.
- EASA: no single EASA TCDS for the FR172 was found; an EASA SAS for an individual FR172H
  (S6) suggests aircraft-specific EASA records. Reims-built 172 types reportedly moved to
  Cessna certificates around 2006 (Cessna Flyer snippet). *Inference; unverified.*
  DGAC records were not searched successfully.
- Sub-models and years (Wikipedia/secondary, **not TCDS**): FR172E 1968, F 1969, G 1970,
  H 1971–72, J 1973–76, K 1977–81. *Unverified.*
- Engines (secondary): early aircraft Continental **IO-360-D/-DB** (e.g. 1970 FR172G
  listing); main civilian Rocket **IO-360-H/-HB, 210 hp**; FR172K (Hawk XP lineage)
  **IO-360-K/-KB, 195 hp at 2,600 rpm**. A 3A17 excerpt gives IO-360-H/-HB at **2,800 rpm
  (210 hp)** for the R172E; R172K POH and Cessna Flyer give 195 hp at 2,600 rpm. Mapping of
  each FR172 letter to engine and rating requires A18EU. *Unverified.*
- Propeller: constant-speed. McCauley catalog lists an R172K/FR172K kit approved under 3A17
  and A18EU with **2A34C203/90DCA-14**, two blades, 76 in maximum diameter, FR172K serial
  range **FR17200591–FR17200675**; a dealer listing gives 90DCA-10 instead. A museum page
  cites C67/C209 propeller interchangeability for FR172H on A18EU. *Unverified; conflict.*
- Maximum takeoff weight **2,550 lb** for the type is reported only by Wikipedia and a sales
  listing; Aeropedia gives 2,500 lb loaded. *Unverified; must come from A18EU per sub-model.*

- `c172x.xml`: "Cessna C-172 Skyhawk II", "Models a 1982 Cessna 172P", engine

## What the TCDS does not provide

TCDS give type-design limits: engine/propeller eligibility, rpm/power limits, airspeed
limits, C.G. range, maximum weights, fuel/oil capacity, control-surface travels, serial
eligibility and certification basis. They do **not** give takeoff/landing/climb/cruise
performance tables, stall speeds by configuration, fuel-flow tables, propeller
efficiency/thrust maps, engine power curves, aerodynamic coefficients, inertias or
operating procedures.

Legitimate sources for those, with status:

- **POH/AFM** (Cessna/Textron copyright): performance tables and procedures. Use as a
  reference for facts and fixture comparison values with citation; do not copy text,
  charts or tables into the repository (R-DATA-1). Applicability to a serial and to a
  revision must be recorded.
- **Open models**: JSBSim aircraft (S13) are approximations derived from public data and
  guesses; usable as a starting structure only, with independent validation against
  sourced performance. License review needed: LGPL-2.1 repository plus per-file "GPL" and
  unlicensed files, and a "not to be sold" note that is an additional restriction
  incompatible with an open license and with Gefly's GPL-3.0-or-later/permissive
  preference (GAPS Q5). Needs a rights decision or upstream clarification before reuse.
- **Published research**: AIAA 82-1312 (S15) and NASA NTRS reports on light-aircraft
  derivatives; NASA works are generally US public domain, AIAA papers are not. Aircraft
  model/year and configuration must be checked before use.
- **Engine manufacturer data**: Lycoming/Continental operator manuals and power charts are
  copyrighted; cite values only.
- **Owned-aircraft flight test**: a future option producing project-owned data.
