### Classic panel versus G1000 (verified from manufacturer manuals, S17/S18)

- The TCDS does **not** state a G1000 serial cut-in. It distinguishes the configurations
  only by flight manual (S1b Note 2; S5 §2 D).
- S18 title page: "Model 172S NAV III Avionics Option, **Serials 172S9810 and On**",
  POH/AFM 172SPHAUS-04, original issue 25 Feb 2005. Therefore factory G1000 ("NAV III")
  172S aircraft begin at s/n 172S9810. *Inference:* every 172S from 172S8001 to 172S9809
  has the conventional panel. Whether all later serials have G1000 is **not established**:
  S18 calls NAV III an "option", so conventional-panel aircraft after 172S9810 may exist.
- S17 (POH/AFM 172SPHUS Rev 5, 19 Jul 2004) covers the conventional 172S from 172S8001.
  Its standard avionics list: Bendix/King KX 155A nav/com with KI 208 or KI 209A indicator,
  KT 76C transponder, KMA 26 audio panel, Pointer 3000-11 ELT (Section 7, p. 7-46).
  Optional supplements include KLN 89/89B GPS, KR 87 ADF and Bendix/King KAP 140 autopilot
  (Section 9 log).
- S17 also records serial-dependent system changes inside the classic range, which affect
  modelling: electrical system changed at **172S8704** (Figure 7-7/7-7A), fuel return
  system from **172S9491** (Section 7 fuel system), KAP 140 roll-steering behaviour from
  172S9423, and items at 172S9129.
- Search snippets (not primary): G1000 172S certified 2005 and announced at AirVenture 2004.

## Reims FR172

Verified:

- S6 (EASA SAS.A.107 Issue 01, p. 2 §1) gives the certification basis of an FR172H as
  **"Type Certificate Data Sheet: FAA A18EU"**, confirming A18EU as the FR172 TCDS. It
  covers one aircraft (FR172H OE-DLS, s/n FR172-00236) with a restricted CofA for hail
  prevention, so it is not a general FR172 specification.
- No EASA TCDS covering the FR172 was found; EASA IM.A.051 (S5) covers only 172R and 172S.
- S10 (IM.E.005 p. 4, table "EASA Type Certification Date", checked visually): the
  **IO-360-J** entry (27 Oct 1972) references **DGAC-F Aircraft TC no. 43 and TCDS no. 117
  (Reims FR172)** (S16). The IO-360-H entry references the Robin HR100-210.
- S10 ratings (p. 7), full throttle, sea-level pressure altitude, minimum values:
  - IO-360-C/-CB/-D/-DB/-E/-G/-GB/-H/-HB/-ES: take-off 157 kW (210 HP) at 2800 rpm; max
    continuous 157 kW (210 HP) at 2800 rpm.
  - IO-360-J/-JB/-K/-KB: take-off 157 kW (210 HP) at 2800 rpm; max continuous 145 kW
    (195 HP) at 2600 rpm.
  - -H differs from -D by crankshaft counterweight tuning; -J and -K differ from -H by
    rating; "B" suffix means modified crankshaft (S10 Notes 4, 8, p. 8).
  - Dry weight 133.4 kg (294 lb) for -A/-D/-DB/-E/-H/-HB/-J/-JB/-K/-KB, without starter and
    alternator (S10 §5 p. 6). Max overspeed 3000 rpm for 10 s (S10 p. 7).

Unverified (secondary sources only; requires A18EU):

- Sub-models and model years: FR172E 1968, F 1969, G 1970, H 1971–72, J 1973–76,
  K 1977–81 (Wikipedia).
- Engine per sub-model: IO-360-D/-DB early; IO-360-H/-HB 210 hp main production;
  FR172K with IO-360-K/-KB limited to 195 hp at 2600 rpm (R172K POH and 3A17 snippets).
  How the aircraft TCDS limits each engine (e.g. whether -J/-K installations use the
  210 hp take-off rating) must come from A18EU.
- Propeller: constant-speed McCauley; kit catalog lists 2A34C203/90DCA-14, 76 in max,
  for FR172K s/n FR17200591–FR17200675; other sources give 90DCA-10. Conflict open.
- Maximum weight: 2,550 lb (Wikipedia, sales listing) vs 2,500 lb (Aeropedia).
- Airspeed limits, C.G., fuel capacity: not found.

## What the TCDS does not provide

TCDS give type-design limits only: engine/propeller eligibility, rpm/power and static-rpm
limits, airspeed limits, C.G., weights, fuel/oil, control travels, serial eligibility and
certification basis. They give no performance tables, stall speeds, fuel-flow tables,
propeller performance maps, engine power curves, aerodynamic coefficients, inertias or
procedures. Sources for those:

- **POH/AFM** (Cessna/Textron copyright): cite individual values with document number,
  revision and page (as above); do not copy text, charts or tables (R-DATA-1).
- **Open models** (S13): structure only, with independent validation. Licence review
  needed: LGPL-2.1 repository, per-file "GPL" and unlicensed files, and a "not to be sold"
  restriction incompatible with an open licence (GAPS Q5).
- **Published research** (S15, NASA NTRS): NASA works are generally US public domain; AIAA
  papers are not. Check aircraft model and configuration first.
- **Engine manuals** (Lycoming/Continental): copyrighted; cite values only.

## Rights summary

- FAA TCDS (S1, S1b, S2, S3, S8, S11): US federal government works, not protected by
  US copyright (17 U.S.C. § 105); quoting and redistributing is generally permitted. Other
  jurisdictions not analysed.
- EASA TCDS/SAS (S5, S6, S9, S10): each page carries "© European Union Aviation Safety
  Agency … All rights reserved" and "Proprietary document. Copies are not controlled."
  Cite facts with references; do not redistribute PDFs. EASA's general website reuse
  terms were not read.
- Cessna POH/information manuals (S17, S18): copyrighted; facts may be cited, no copying.
  Publicly hosted copies are not licensed.
- JSBSim (S13): see above.

## Gaps and uncertainties

1. **A18EU not read.** FR172 sub-models, engines, propellers, weights and speeds stay
   unverified. Options: a DRS API key (DRS offers registered API access), a manual
   download by a person, or DGAC TCDS no. 117 (S16).
2. Current FAA 3A12 Rev 88 not read directly; S5 claims alignment with it. Date of Rev 88
   unknown. 3A17, 1E10 and E1CE not read.
3. Classic-panel aircraft built after 172S9810 (if any) are not identified.
4. Discrepancies: Vfe CAS 85 kt (S1b) vs 84 kt (S5); utility max ramp 2,208 lb (S1b) vs
   2,107 lb (S5). Resolve against 3A12 Rev 88.
5. Serial-dependent classic 172S systems (172S8704 electrical, 172S9491 fuel return)
   require choosing a serial sub-range.
6. Optional avionics (GPS, autopilot) vary by aircraft; the standard list in S17 is the
   defensible baseline.
7. JSBSim licence compatibility and model mismatches.

## Recommendation (proposal for product/orchestrator)

First configuration:

- **Cessna 172S, FAA TCDS 3A12 (verify against Rev 88), normal category, conventional
  panel, serial range 172S9491–172S9809**: after the fuel-return and later electrical
  changes, before the NAV III (G1000) option at 172S9810. Flight manual reference:
  POH/AFM 172SPHUS, Revision 5 (19 Jul 2004). A wider 172S8001–172S9809 range is also
  defensible if serial-specific systems are not modelled.
- Engine Lycoming IO-360-L2A, 180 hp, 2,700 rpm. McCauley 1A170E/JHA7660 fixed-pitch
  propeller, static rpm 2,300–2,400, 75–76 in.
- Limits: 2,550 lb; VA 105, VNO 129, VNE 163, VFE 85 KIAS; C.G. per 3A12.
- Avionics: the S17 standard fit (KX 155A nav/com with KI 208/209A, KT 76C, KMA 26). Label
  the optional KAP 140/KLN 89B as not modelled, or as separate optional configurations.
- Performance acceptance: cited S17 introduction values as initial targets; detailed
  checks against the applicable POH tables by reference only.

Second configuration: decide the FR172 sub-model only after reading A18EU. Candidates,
not a decision: FR172K (IO-360-K/-KB, Hawk XP counterpart) or FR172H/J (210 hp IO-360-H
family). Note from S10 that the IO-360-J was validated in Europe through the Reims FR172
DGAC TC.

Next checks: obtain A18EU and 3A12 Rev 88 (DRS API key or manual download), then 1E10,
E1CE and DGAC TCDS 117. Resolve gaps 1–4 before creating any fixture.
